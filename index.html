Field manual

Shapes join into units based on how they touch. Formations are spotted the moment you place them, so watch the tags.

    How a game runs

    1. Each side gets 5 squares, 4 triangles, 3 circles on its own half of an 8×8 grid.
    2. Blue places 1 block, then each side places 2 at a time. Placements are visible.
    3. Squares are grouped first into castles, then columns (trucks), then rows (walls). Leftovers are buggies.
    4. Each turn, everything fires at once, then everything moves at once. Two units heading for the same cell both wait.
    5. 1 point per damage, +5 per kill. Riders lost with their buggy count as kills.
    6. The battle lasts 20 turns, or ends early if a side is wiped out or 3 turns pass with nothing happening.
